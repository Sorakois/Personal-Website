Nurture, a community helping gamers!

Nurture is a community-focused server dedicated to helping players progress! Catering mainly towards Cookie Run: Kingdom and Honkai: Star Rail players, Nurture offers personalized help with your issues, whether it be game-related or not.
Nurture offers:
A safe and welcoming community; Tips! (There will be nothing you won't know once you're in our server!); A great place to seek growth - whether it be in-game or anywhere else; Game Leaks; Public Voice Calls; Occasional giveaways; A supportive and growing community; ... and so much more!

Azalea, Nurture's Discord Bot!

Azalea brings knowledge and entertainment to Nurture. Users can look up information about any character in Cookie Run: Kingdom or Honkai: Star Rail, do "gacha" to collect characters from Cookie Run: Kingdom, and so many more miscellaneous commands! Azalea is programmed entirely using Python, but additionally uses SQL for things like keeping track of a user's inventory, and HTML to scrape information off the web.
